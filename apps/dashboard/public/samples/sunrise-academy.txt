Sunrise Academy - Information for students and parents
(Fictional business used for the BotForge demo.)

ABOUT
Sunrise Academy is a coaching institute on FC Road, Pune, preparing students for JEE Main, JEE Advanced and NEET since 2011.
More than 1,200 of our students have been selected for IITs, NITs and government medical colleges.
Batches are small: at most 30 students per batch. Weekly tests every Sunday morning, doubt-solving every evening from 7 PM to 8 PM.
Phone: +91 20 0000 0000 (demo number). Office hours: Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM. Sunday 10 AM to 1 PM.

COURSES AND FEES (2026-27)
JEE Main + Advanced, 2-year programme: Rs 68,000 per year.
NEET, 2-year programme: Rs 62,000 per year.
Foundation course for Class 8, 9 and 10, 1-year programme: Rs 38,000 per year.
Fees can be paid in 3 instalments: at admission, in October and in January.
Study material and test series are included in the fee. GST is extra.

SCHOLARSHIPS
Our scholarship test (Sunrise Talent Test) is held on the first Sunday of every month.
Scholarship by rank: top 10 students get 50% off the fee, rank 11 to 50 get 25% off, rank 51 to 100 get 10% off.
Children of defence personnel get an additional 10% off.

BATCHES AND TIMINGS
The next JEE and NEET batches start on 5 January.
Morning batch: 7 AM to 10 AM. Evening batch: 4 PM to 7 PM. Classes run Monday to Saturday.
Foundation batches run on weekdays from 5 PM to 7 PM.

FREE DEMO CLASS
Every Sunday at 11 AM anyone can attend a free demo class. Please register your name and phone number so the counsellor can reserve a seat.

ADMISSION PROCESS
1. Attend a free demo class or the scholarship test.
2. Meet the counsellor and choose a batch.
3. Submit the admission form with 2 photos, a copy of the latest marksheet and the first instalment.
Refund policy: full refund within 15 days of admission, 50% refund within 30 days, no refund after that.

FACILITIES
Air-conditioned classrooms, a library open from 8 AM to 9 PM, online recordings of every lecture, and a parent app with test results.
Hostel tie-ups are available near the institute for outstation students, starting at Rs 9,000 per month including meals.
