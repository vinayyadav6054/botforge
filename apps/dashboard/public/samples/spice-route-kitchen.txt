ABOUT
Spice Route Kitchen is a family restaurant in Koramangala 5th Block, Bengaluru, serving North Indian, South Indian and Indo-Chinese food. Pure vegetarian kitchen; Jain food on request.
Seating for 80 guests, air-conditioned, wheelchair accessible. Phone / WhatsApp: +91 80 0000 0000 (demo number).
