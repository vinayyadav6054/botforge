# Getting clients with no budget

The product is good enough to sell. What sells it is showing an owner **their own business** answering a real
customer question, and a story people repeat. This is the whole path from "nobody knows us" to "they paid".

## 1. Pick one niche and one city first

Start with **coaching institutes in your own city**. Their pain is sharp and easy to show: parents ask about fees,
batches and demo classes all day and late at night, and one admission (₹40,000–₹70,000) pays for years of Kahiye.
Once 3 of them pay, add clinics, then real-estate agents. One niche at a time makes every message, demo and
testimonial reusable.

## 3. The funnel and the numbers to watch

```
Prospect list ─▶ Personal demo sent ─▶ Reply ─▶ Free trial live ─▶ First lead received ─▶ Paid
     100               30                10            5                    4                2
```

Track each prospect in a Google Sheet with these columns. Each week, look at where people stop and fix that step
(better demos if few reply; faster setup if trials do not go live; check-ins if trials do not convert).

## 4. Seven free ways to reach owners

1. **Warm network (week 1).** People who know you: your old coaching class, relatives' clinics, friends' parents'
   shops. Ask for honest feedback, not a sale. Your first user and testimonial usually comes from here.
2. **"I built this for you" demos.** Pick businesses from Google Maps with many reviews. Build their bot from public
   information (15 minutes), then send the demo link and the video on WhatsApp or Instagram. A demo with their own name
   gets far more replies than any pitch.
3. **Short videos (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts).** Record your screen: *"I asked this coaching institute's
   website a question at 11 PM. Here's what happened."* One a day for 30 days. Show real answers in Hinglish; end
   with "Want one for your business? Comment DEMO."
4. **Web designers and freelancers.** They build sites for local businesses and want something to upsell. Offer them
   **25% of every payment for 12 months** for each client they bring. One partner can bring 10 clients.
5. **Local groups.** City business groups on WhatsApp and Facebook, coaching associations, chemist and clinic groups.
   Do not spam: post one helpful tip ("3 questions parents ask after 9 PM") with the video, then answer replies.
6. **Google Business Profile** for Kahiye (free), so "AI chatbot for coaching institute [city]" finds you.
7. **Referrals.** Every paying client gets **one month free for each business they refer** that pays.

## 5. From reply to payment

1. **Reply:** send the demo link and video; offer to set it up for free during the 14-day trial.
2. **Setup (you do it):** upload their brochure, connect the website or Instagram bio link, switch on lead alerts to
   their phone's email, add their UPI and booking links.
3. **Check-ins:** day 3 ("first questions came in, here they are"), day 7 (fix any wrong answers together), day 12
   (show the numbers: questions answered, leads, how many after office hours).
4. **Ask:** *"In 12 days it answered 84 questions and brought 9 parents' numbers, 5 of them after 8 PM. To keep it,
   it's ₹1,499 a month."* Founder offer for the first 10 clients: **₹999/month for 3 months** if they pay this week.
5. **Payment:** they pay by UPI from *Plan & account* (QR code) and send the reference; you check your bank app and
   switch the plan on in `/admin`. No gateway, no fees.

## 6. Objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We already reply on WhatsApp." | "Who replies at 11 PM or on Sunday? Parents ask the next institute while they wait." |
| "AI gives wrong answers." | "It answers only from your documents and says when it doesn't know. You see every chat and can correct any answer in one click." |
| "Too costly." | "Less than one day of a receptionist's salary. One admission pays for two years." |
| "Let me think." | "It's free for 14 days and I set it up for you. Decide when you've seen the leads." |

## 8. The story people share

Products get bought; stories get shared. Yours has three true parts. Write them in your own words, with real
details only:

1. **The moment:** a real time you or someone you know waited for an answer from a business, or a business that
   lost a customer because nobody replied.
2. **Why you:** you are a developer in India who saw local businesses lose enquiries every night, and built the
   receptionist they cannot afford to hire.
3. **The first win:** after your first client, a real number: *"Sunrise got 9 enquiries in two weeks, 5 after
   office hours."* (Only with their permission.)

Use it on the website's "Why Kahiye exists" section, in your first post, and at the start of every demo call.

## 9. Money and paperwork (simple version)

- Start with UPI to your own account; keep a simple record of every payment.
- GST registration becomes mandatory for services once yearly turnover crosses ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in some states);
  you can also register earlier voluntarily. Ask a CA when you approach that.
- Before moving to Razorpay subscriptions, you will need KYC (PAN, bank account, a website with policies, which you
  now have).
