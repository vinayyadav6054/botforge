# Demo kit: a personal demo for a business in 15 minutes

The strongest sales move is an owner chatting with an assistant that already knows **their** business.

## Before you start (once)

- Live site working, with UptimeRobot keeping Render awake.
- In Vercel, set `NEXT_PUBLIC_CONTACT_WHATSAPP` (your WhatsApp number with 91) and `NEXT_PUBLIC_PAYMENT_UPI`
  (your UPI ID) so the site and payment page show them.
- The intro video is on the home page and at `/media/kahiye-intro.mp4`. Download it once to your phone.

## Make the demo (15 minutes)

1. **Collect public information** (5 min): Google Maps listing (timings, address, reviews), website, Instagram posts,
   Justdial. Paste it into a text file: about, services/courses with prices, timings, address, how to book, 10 common
   questions with answers. Never invent prices; leave out what you do not know.
   No time? Start from the matching sample in `/samples/` and change the name, address and prices.
2. **Create the assistant** (3 min): in your own account, *New bot* → their business name. Upload the file.
3. **Make it look like theirs** (3 min): Settings → Basics & look: their colour and logo, a welcome line, 4 suggested
   questions. Leads & alerts: on. Booking & payment: their booking or WhatsApp link if public.
4. **Test it** (3 min): in *Test & answers*, ask 10 questions in English, Hindi and Hinglish. Correct anything wrong.
5. **Copy the demo link**: Install → Demo link.

## Send it

WhatsApp (or Instagram DM), then attach the intro video:

> Namaste! I made a free AI assistant for **[Business name]**. It answers your customers' questions about fees,
> timings and admissions 24x7, from your own information, in Hindi or English, and sends you the number of everyone who
> wants a call back. Try it here: **[demo link]**. If you like it, I will put it on your website and Instagram free for
> 14 days.

Follow up once after 2 days: *"Did you get a chance to try it? Ask it anything a parent would ask."*

## Show it in person or on a call (5 minutes)

1. Hand them your phone with the demo link open; let **them** ask a question.
2. Ask a Hinglish question yourself (*"fees kitni hai?"*).
3. Tap the call-back button, leave a test number, then show the lead arriving in your dashboard.
4. Show *Conversations* → *Take over*: "you can always step in yourself".
5. Offer the free 14-day setup.

## After they say yes

Move the assistant to their own account: they sign up (or you sign them up with their email), you upload the same
file there, and you delete the demo copy from your account. Their customers' data then belongs to their account.
