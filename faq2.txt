Smile Care is open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Free parking is available behind the clinic. The nearest railway station is a 5 minute walk away.

We accept most major health insurance plans and also offer easy EMI options for treatments above 20000 INR.
